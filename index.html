Sangwoo Han

Postdoctoral Researcher, University of Washington, Seattle

sangwooh@uw.edu · GitHub

I am a seismologist and a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Washington, working with Marin Denolle and Paul Bodin on multi-hazard, multi-sensor monitoring at Mount Rainier. I received my Ph.D. in Seismology and Geophysics from Seoul National University in 2026. My research uses dense seismic arrays and distributed acoustic sensing (DAS) to resolve fault structure and earthquake rupture complexity at sub-kilometer scales.

Research interests

  • Rupture processes and source complexity of small earthquakes
  • Distributed acoustic sensing and dense nodal / broadband arrays
  • Focal mechanisms from S-wave polarization and stress inversion
  • Microearthquake detection and location
  • Multi-sensor hazard monitoring at Mount Rainier

Methods

Rupture process analysis, EGF analysis, slip inversion, back-projection, focal mechanisms, stress inversion, microseismicity detection and location, waveform modeling, DAS, receiver functions
